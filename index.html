About

I am a PhD student in the Natural Language Processing Research Group at the University of Sheffield. I am supervised by Dr Xi Wang, Lecturer (~Assistant Professor) in Natural Language Processing at the University of Sheffield, and Dr Shuo Zhang, Senior Research Scientist at Bloomberg AI in London, UK. Before then, I received my bachelor's degree from Nanjing Normal University and my master's degree from Renmin University of China. My research interests are conversational recommender systems and information retrieval.
